  9. Null: The population percentages are the same. Alternative: The population percentages are different.
    For the treatment group, the box SD =.342, and the percent SE is about 1.54%. For the control group, the SD is .285 and the SE is about 1.28%. The SE of the difference is therefore about sqrt(1.54^2 + 1.28^2) = 2.00%. The observed difference is (13.54 - 8.89) = 4.65%, and the expected difference under the null is 0. The z-score is 2.32, and the p value is about 2%. (We asked for 2-sided, so don't divide by 2. We want both tails.) Reject the null. The treatment probably has a real (harmful) effect.

    You can also do this as a chi-square test of independence, which gives a chi-square statistic of about 5.36 and 1 d.f., for a p-value between 1% and 5%, for the same conclusions.
