    1. The weights of the sticks are drawn at random from a box; the box has an average of 4 ounces, and the SD is 0.05 ounces. The weight of a package is like the sum of 4 draws. The expected value is 4*4 = 16 ounces. The SE for the sum is sqrt{4}*0.05 = 0.1 ounces.
    2. The total weight of 100 packages is like the sum of 400 draws form the box. The expected value is 400*4 = 1600 ounces = 100 pounds. The SE for the sum is sqrt{400}*0.05 = 1 ounce. The total weight will be 100 pounds , give or take 1 ounce or so. The range ``100 pounds plus or minus 2 ounces'' is ``expected value plus or minus 2 SEs'', so the chance is about 95%.
