This means: below 48%: F, above 48%: D, above 54%: D+, above 62%: C-, above 66%: C, above 71%: C+, above 75%: B-, above 79%: B, above 84%: B+, above 88%: A-, and above 90%: A. I typically awarded the next higher grade if someone was within 1 to 3 points of the next highest cutoff, e.g., 898 = 89.8% was still an A (and not an A-).
