USU Catalog Description

Exams

There are two midterms and one final exam. The midterms are worth 200 points each. The final exam is worth 450 points.

All exams are open-book, open-notes, open-computer (i.e., you have full access to CyberStats and all its tools, calculators, etc.). The two midterms should be completed in 60 min (1 hour) and the final in 180 min (3 hours). You are expected to use the CyberStats tools, calculators, etc. efficiently. If you rarely looked at the CyberStats tools and only read the main text, you will most likely fail the exams.

Midterm 1 will be given in week 7 (Monday 2/23/04) and will cover the following 11 subunits: A1-A8, and B1-B3. Exact rooms and times for Midterm 1 will be posted early in the semester.

Midterm 2 will be given in week 11 (Monday 3/22/04) and will cover the following 9 subunits: B5-B7, B-9, B11-B13, and C1-C2. Exact rooms and times for Midterm 2 will be posted early in the semester.

The final exam will be given in week 15 (Monday 4/19/04) and will cover the following 28 subunits: A1-A8, B1-B3, B5-B7, B-9, B11-B13, C1-C4, C6-C8, C10, and D1-D2 (i.e., the final is comprehensive). Subunits D-3 and E-1 will not be assessed in the final exam although they are part of the overall course and the corresponding homework solutions have to be turned in by the end of week 15. Exact rooms and times for the final exam will be posted early in the semester.

Exam questions will be at the same difficulty level as the exercises from the homework assignments. In fact, some exam questions will be drawn from the remaining exercise sets (2, 3, ...). The final exam will consist of regular questions and a data part where you are given a real data set and are required to answer questions using the CyberStats tools. The two midterms and the final exam will be graded by your US instructor. Sample questions (with answers) will be provided later in the semester.
