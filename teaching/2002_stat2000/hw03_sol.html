    The boxplots for this data set look OK. But some warning - WebStat does not produce the modified version of the boxplot we introduced in class, i.e., outliers are not labeled with a `*' symbol. The lines outside the box really stretch to the min and to the max - no matter whether these are below Q1 - 1.5 x IQR or above Q3 + 1.5 x IQR. Check yourself by adding some small or large value (e.g., -500 or 500) to the data set.
