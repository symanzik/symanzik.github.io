Homework 6 - Part II

Ann Kitchen

Evaluations of lectures on introductory topics of statistics presented in class.

 

1. Bruce and Glen- Basic Graphics

  • Bruce provided a good introduction into the talk, and I thought it was a good idea to focus on two topics as they did (histograms and boxplots), instead of trying to cover all graphics since time was limited for the lecture.
  • It was somewhat distracting to have one person playing with the computer and writing on the board when the other person was presenting material. However, as the allotted time to the lecture was only 18 minutes, I realize that there was little else that could be done.
  • The histogram was described both on the board and then again using the picture in the web-site. This seemed somewhat redundant- one would probably have been enough.
  • The use of the interactive histogram was very good.
  • The description of the boxplot was very good.
  • It was good to have the handout and additional information sources.
  • I would have liked to have seen more interaction with the class.
  • Overall, I think the use of web-based tools were effective, and that the presentation was enhanced by their use.

2. Qian and JingJing- Summary Statistics

  • The introduction was well thought out and presented, however, in the time allowed, I thought it was a bit too long- it would have been better to spend more time on the concepts.
  • I had trouble following along as I did not have time to type in the URL before the presenter had already moved along.
  • A substantial proportion of the lecture could have been done without use of the computer. The concepts were described very well, but for this part, the lecture was similar to a classic ‘chalk-and-board’ lecture, except using the text that was already in the web-site.
  • The interactive applet was well used.
  • Overall, I think the lecture was enhanced by the use of web-based tools, although I think the web-site was overused- only a small proportion of the lecture (ie. the applet) really need to be done on the computer.


3. Gary- Confidence intervals

  • I thought the use of an applied example to introduce the topic was excellent (USAToday).
  • The presenter was very practiced and smooth at presenting concepts and speaking- that facilitated the lecture.
  • There was good use of, and explanation of the calculation of confidence intervals on the computer.
  • I would like to have seen a bit more graphical representation of confidence intervals using the web-based tools.
  • Overall, the concepts were presented well, and the web-based tools increased the effectiveness of the presentation.

4. Weiping- Linear Regression

  • The introduction was good, and the concepts were described very well.
  • The interactive tool was very good, and used well, although a lot of time was spent on it, and I would have liked to see a few more examples or interactive tools about how linear regression works and is used.
  • Overall, this was a good presentation, and I think the use of web-based tools did help in facilitating understanding of the concepts.
