Evaluations for the presentations of web-based statistical software packages.

  • Webstats

First, they talked about the overall features of webstats, and then showed us in detail how to read data, perform the simple linear-regression, plot, compute summary statistics, and get the one-way ANOVA.

Basically, I understand the methods of using this package presented in class. I can work with most steps following the lecturers. When I was lost in replacing the data, they helped me individually. The amount of material is OK during 45-50 minutes. After their presentation, it seems easy to use this package. I like their detailed and helpful on-line lecture page.

I can do most of the homework quickly based on the material presented in class.

  • Statlets

They opened the homepage of Statlets to show us the various features, and talked about how to use the web-based package, including loading the data and analyzing the data.

I understand how to use this package at the end of this lecture. Actually, at first, I am confused about loading data and choosing which buttons to do analysis. With their individual help and some practice in class, I can do it finally. The lecturer showed us how to get the results step by step. I liked to click the options to see some changes under their directions. The materials are OK during 45-50 minutes.

The printout is useful, because it tells us the steps to use the package. I can finish the homework easily after reading the printout.
