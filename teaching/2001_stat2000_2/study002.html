Stat 2000, Section 002, Study Guide for the Final

    • Graphics: Histograms, Boxplots, Micromaps, Bad (Newspaper) Graphics, Outliers in Graphics, Scatterplots & Scatterplot Matrices, Linked Brushing
    • Straight Lines, Linear Regression, Correlation, Residual Plots, Prediction
    • Samples, Populations, Experiments, Studies, SRS, Other Sampling Mechanisms
    • Probability, Conditional Probability, Independence, Probability Trees
    • Reading and Interpreting Computer Output
    • Simple Hand Calculations & Use of Tables (in Case that CyberStats is not Working)
