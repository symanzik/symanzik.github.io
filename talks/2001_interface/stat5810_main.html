  3. You can now sign up for 2 or 3 credits. Initially, this course was just offered as a 2 credit course plus the possibility to sign up for one more credit as "Directed Reading". Due to curriculum requirements for some of our students, we now offer this course as a full 3 credit course as well.

    If you already signed up (or plan to sign up) for 2 credits, we will do exactly the same things as planned previously and outlined in the Stat 5810 Syllabus. However, if you plan to sign up for 3 credits, you have to work on an extended project that hopefully will lead to a (conference proceedings) paper. Possible topics for this project/paper are the numerical performance of statistical packages on the Web or the comparative evaluation of Web-based teaching tools. We will decide which topic(s) to pursue once it is clear how many people will select the 3 credit option. Please contact me in case of any questions regarding the 2 or 3 credit option. (4/17/2000)
