Final survey
-----------

Finally, here is the last survey related to our
'Web-based Statistics' course. It is optional to
reply to this survey, but please be aware that=20
your feedback is essential for the quality of
future courses. So please look back at the
Stat 5810 course, recall your experiences and
feelings about the course, and try to answer
the following questions as openly and
honestly as possible (some of the questions
have already been discussed during our
last official meeting, so try to recall and
write down what you said or thought at that time).=20

Again, your answers will have absolutely=20
no influence on your grade,
but they will certainly have influence on the
quality of future offerings of this course.

Here are the questions. Just type your answer
below each question and e-mail them to=20
symanzik@sunfs.math.usu.edu.=20

1. On average, how many hours a day/week
did you spend on browsing the Web, writing
the reports, and other preparation for
this course? Was it more or less time=20
than you previously expected, or just
as you expected?

2. Which kind of activities (writing reports,
class presentation, project etc.)
did you find most useful, interesting,
or challenging?=20

3. Which kind of activities did you find
most useless, bothering, or boring?

4. How much time (approximately) did
you invest into your project?

5. Did you find any difficulties regarding
the realization of the project?

6. What kind of knowledge or information
aquired in this course is most useful
and applicable to your area of=20
work/study/research?

7. What kind of knowledge could you
apply in your own area right now?=20
What kind of knowledge would need
some 'polishing' before you could
successfully apply it in practice?

8. Is there any point in the syllabus
that should be discussed in more details,
added, or left out from the syllabus?

9. Would you further recommend this
course? If so, to whom would you
recommend it?

Thank you for your answers. If you
have any other comments, ideas, or
tips, please feel free to mention
them now.
