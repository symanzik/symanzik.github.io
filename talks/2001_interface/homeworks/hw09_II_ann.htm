Homework 9 Part II:

Evaluation of presentations of web-based statistical programs.

  1. Presentation on Statlets

    Roxanne and Glen

    • Overall, I found the presentation good; it was well-prepared and taught.
    • There was a very good introduction to the site, and what was available (e.g. revisions, instructors information etc.)
    • I found that I did understand how to use the package, and that an appropriate amount of material was presented for the class to work through to understand the package.
    • The homework was designed well; I was able to complete it quite easily with the information that I obtained during the class.
    • The one downfall of the presentation, I thought, was the technical difficulties that took up some time during the lecture, but these were things that the presenters had little control over and thus they were not their fault.


  2. Presentation on XploRe

    3. Weiping and Jingjing

    • The presentation overall was of a high quality, but I found that the overview of the site left me somewhat lost. I felt that the features of the site were not explained fully (e.g. what language the program works in). I think it would have been better to discuss the ‘basic features of XploRe’ at the beginning of the lecture, and not at the end.
    • The presentation of the actual execution of the package I thought was handled very well considering that this package was more complex than the others and involved using a language. I obtained a good introduction on how the package works during this section of the presentation.
    • The presentation was timed well, with not too much or too little information being discussed.
    • I felt that the exercises and the homework were a little too easy to teach us very much about the package or how it runs.
